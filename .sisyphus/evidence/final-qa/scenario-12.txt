CONDITIONAL RENDERING LOGIC
----------------------------
The UI uses nested conditional rendering to show/hide sections:

1. News Preferences section only visible when:
   editing ? form.agentic_context_opt_in : (profile as any).agentic_context_opt_in

2. News Topics and Cadence only visible when:
   editing ? form.news_enabled : (profile as any).news_enabled

This ensures the UI adapts to both editing and viewing states correctly.
